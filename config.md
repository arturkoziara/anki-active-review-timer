- **idle_seconds**: stop counting after this many seconds with no key press, click, scroll or new card.
- **reset_after_minutes**: timer goes back to 0:00 after this long without review activity (your break).
- **pomodoro_minutes**: show a break reminder after this much active review. Set to 0 to turn off.

Restart Anki after changing these.
- **break_minutes**: break timer turns green and reminds you after this long. Set to 0 to turn off.
